Type what you know into the search box: an artist, a title, a label, a catalog number, or a mix. Results come as you type, from every shop at once; Enter takes you to them.

## What to type

- **Artist and title**: `moodymann black mahogani`. Word order doesn't matter, and the last word can be cut short: `moodymann black maho` works.
- **Catalog numbers**: `KDJ-38`, `KDJ 38` and `kdj38` all find the same record. Shops often put the catalog number in the title or the product code, so it's the most precise search there is.
- **Labels**: `planet e`, `warp`. Shops don't always name the label, so a label search finds part of its catalog.
- **Typos**: when nothing matches, the search suggests the closest spelling, or the search without the word that matched nothing.
- **Shops, artists, cities and countries**: a shop's name (`red eye records`), an artist with a page (`pink floyd animals`), a city with a page (`wien`, `londres`) or a country, in your language or in English (`allemagne`, `germany`), adds a link to its page above the records.

## Reading the results

Each row is one record, with every copy of it across shops grouped together: "from €24 · 6 shops". Open a row to compare the copies: price, condition (new or used, with the grade), format, the shop and its country. The links go straight to the shop's page for that copy.

- **Prices in your currency**: prices are converted with the day's European Central Bank rates ("≈ €24"), the shop's own price is shown underneath. Choose the currency in Filters or in More.
- **Sold out**: records that sold out recently stay visible, marked, with the day they were last in stock, so you know a shop carries them.
- **Your country first**: copies in your country, then in your customs zone (no import fees), come first when you ask for it in Filters.

## Filters

On phones, the Filters button at the bottom of the results; on wide screens, the column on the left:

- **In stock only**
- **Your country first**
- **Format**: LP, 12", 7", 10", CD, cassette, as the shops name them
- **Prices in**: your currency, or each shop's own
- **Show covers**: off saves data on slow connections

## Sharing

The address of the page is the search: copy it, or use Share in a record's detail, to send someone a search or a record with all its copies.
