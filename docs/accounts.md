## No account

LP/EP Finder has no accounts. Your list (the records you watch, your Discogs lists, your radio, your filters) is kept in your browser, nowhere else. Nothing about you is stored on our side.

- **Another device**: **Settings › Move to another device › Download my list** makes a file; open it in the other browser with **Open a file**. Opening a file adds its records to the list there.
- **A Discogs wantlist** can also be imported again by its username on any device.
- **Share my watchlist** (Me › ⋯) makes a link with your records, for someone else or another device.
- **Clearing your browser's site data** clears the list: download it first if you want to keep it.

## Profiles and Bluesky, until 2026-11-22

Until 22 November 2026, a browser could hold several profiles, and a profile could sign in to a Bluesky account to sync its list and publish it. Both were removed: nobody had published a list, and they made the site harder to use.

- **Several profiles**: the first one is now your list. The others are kept in your browser until you decide: the **Me** page lists them, each with **Download**, **Merge into my list** and **Remove**.
- **Signed in with Bluesky**: this browser forgot the sign-in. The copy of your list in your Bluesky account stays there until you delete it: its records are in the collections whose names start with `com.lpepfinder.` (a record manager such as [pdsls.dev](https://pdsls.dev) can delete them).
