LP/EP Finder searches the catalogs of independent record shops: millions of records in stock, in one search, with prices in your currency and a link to the shop that sells each copy. No account, no tracking cookies, no marketplace: you buy from the shop.

## Using LP/EP Finder

- [Searching](searching.md): artists, titles, labels, catalog numbers, and what the results show.
- [Dig](dig.md): what's new in the shops, back in stock or cheaper this week.
- [Your watchlist](watchlist.md): bring your Discogs wantlist, see which records shops have now.
- [For record shops](for-shops.md): get listed, update your listing, or opt out.

## How it's made

- [How it works](how-it-works.md): from a list of shops to a search engine, every day, on free services.
- [Data formats](data-formats.md): the files the browser reads.
- [Offer ledger](ledger.md): the history behind new, back in stock and price drops.
- [Development](development.md): commands, code style, navigation, translations.
- [CI](ci.md): the daily refresh and the deploy.
- [Editing data](editing-data.md): shops, settings and texts.

The site's code and these docs live on [GitHub](https://github.com/lpepfinder/lpepfinder.github.io). Questions and ideas are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/lpepfinder/lpepfinder.github.io/issues).
