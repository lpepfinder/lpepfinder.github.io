Dig is the record bin of the whole network: this week's records, as covers to flip through. Tap a cover to go to the record at its shop.

## The crates

- **Your records**: records on your [watchlist](watchlist.md) that are new, back in stock or cheaper this week. Shown when you have a watchlist.
- **New**: records the shops added in the last 7 days. Shopify shops say when they added a product; for other shops, a record is new when our daily crawl sees it for the first time (from a shop's third crawl, so a newly listed shop's whole catalog doesn't count as new).
- **Back in stock**: records that sold out and came back.
- **Price drops**: records at least 10% cheaper after a recent drop. Drops of more than 70% are left out: they are almost always a pricing mistake, not a deal.

Shops take turns in every crate, so one shop's big upload doesn't fill it.

## Filters

- **Ships to me**: hides shops known not to ship to your country.
- **No customs**: only shops in your country or your customs zone (the EU, for example), so no import fees.
- **Format**: LP, 12", 7"…

Your filters are kept in this browser. The crates are built once a day, after the night's crawl.
